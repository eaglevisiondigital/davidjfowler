WHO I AM

Built to lead. Called to serve.

I’m David J. Fowler — most people simply know me as Dave. I’m a husband, dad, pastor, coach, entrepreneur, author and builder.

Leadership and coaching have also been a significant part of my story. I spent seven years coaching college basketball, helping build championship-level teams and being part of three national championships. Those years reinforced lessons I still carry today about culture, preparation, accountability, developing people and helping a team believe it can reach a level it has never reached before.

For years, I have known that my life would carry a blend of ministry and business. Ministry is not an accessory to what we build; it is a major reason we build. At the same time, I believe God has given us practical ideas, technology, companies and platforms that can genuinely help people and organizations increase in every arena of life.

Whether someone meets me through a revival, a church platform, a website, a business solution, a children’s book, a voiceover project or a global ministry initiative, the heartbeat is the same: help people see farther, move forward and become more of what God created them to be.
